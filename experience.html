THE BEETLES & BUTTERFLIES EXPERIENCE

Four to six weeks to discover, develop, practice, and demonstrate your next level.

More than a workshop. A developmental experience designed to build self-awareness, confidence, leadership capability, and practical skills—so you can grow, lead, and navigate what comes next with intention and impact.

What participants can expect

Registered participants begin their journey with a personalized toolkit that includes a brief orientation, acknowledgement forms, and other information on how to get the most from their experience. Participants then meet once a week for instruction, guided reflection, discussion, hands-on activities, peer learning, and opportunities to practice new skills in realistic situations.

There will be one virtual meeting during the 4-week program with all participants, to answer questions, reinforce learning, and provide additional guidance between sessions.

The experience concludes with a participant presentation summarizing what they learned, how they grew, and how they plan to apply their learning moving forward. Parents are welcome to attend the final presentation.
