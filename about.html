WHO WE ARE

At the heart of Beetles & Butterflies is a commitment to developing the next generation of confident, purpose-driven leaders, people equipped to navigate change, lead with intention, and create meaningful impact.

We believe some of life’s greatest growth happens during moments of transition. From high school to college, from college to the workforce, from individual contributor to leader, or simply from where you are to where you know you want to be, those moments can bring uncertainty, possibility, and transformation. We help people navigate those moments with strength, confidence, and purpose.

The Beetle

The beetle symbolizes strength, resilience, persistence, determination and the courage to keep moving.

The Butterfly

The butterfly symbolizes growth, evolution, and a reminder that change is not the end of who we are but rather the beginning of who we are capable of becoming.
