PARTICIPANT EXPECTATIONS

Participant Code of Conduct

Our goal is to create a learning environment where participants feel safe, respected, challenged, supported, and responsible for their own growth.

Respectful Communication

Speak to others with respect. Listen without interrupting and disagree with ideas without attacking people.

Mobile Phones & Devices

Mobile phones are not permitted during workshops unless a facilitator gives permission for a learning activity or an urgent need arises. Phones should be silenced and stored away.

Engagement & Preparation

Participate in discussions and activities, ask questions, practice new skills, and complete assigned preparation and learning activities.

Time & Attendance

Arrive on time, remain for the full session, and communicate in advance when an absence or delay is unavoidable.

Confidentiality

Respect personal stories and experiences shared by participants. Do not repeat another participant’s personal information without permission.

Inclusion & Belonging

Respect differences in background, culture, race, ethnicity, gender, beliefs, experience, and ability. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, or intimidation are not acceptable.

Professional Conduct

Use good judgment in language, behavior, attire, and interactions. Treat the workshop as a professional development environment.

Ownership & Accountability

Take responsibility for your choices, learning, and commitments. Be willing to receive feedback, reflect honestly, and make adjustments.

Technology & Recording

Do not photograph, record, livestream, or share workshop materials or another participant’s comments without facilitator permission.

Safety

Follow facilitator instructions and report concerns that could affect the safety or well-being of yourself or another participant.

Consequences

Conduct concerns may be addressed through coaching, a warning, removal from an activity, or dismissal from the program when behavior is serious or repeated.

Participant & Parent/Guardian Acknowledgment

By signing below, we acknowledge that we have reviewed the Beetles & Butterflies Code of Conduct and understand the expectations for participation.
